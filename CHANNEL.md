# Politics of Everything

## Channel identity
- Language: English
- Audience: International, with special focus on India
- Format: evidence-led documentary explainers
- Length: 10–15 minutes
- Delivery: one completed video every Sunday
- Editorial authority: Editor-in-Chief (human)
- Publishing: disabled; no upload or public release without explicit human approval

## Editorial promise
Explain how power works: who holds it, how institutions shape it, how money and ideas influence decisions, and how political choices affect ordinary life. Make complex systems understandable without flattening disagreement or manufacturing controversy.

## Coverage
- Political institutions, constitutions, democracy, elections and governance
- Political economy, markets, inequality, labour and public finance
- International relations, geopolitics, war, diplomacy and global institutions
- Society, culture, identity, movements and political thought
- Technology, platforms, surveillance, AI and power
- Indian politics and public institutions in comparative/global context
